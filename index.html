The ecosystem

One loop: research, incubator, classroom

The courses do not invent problems from scratch. They draw on what CUD already has.

Research as textbooks and data

CUD faculty books and the Case Study Lab supply local cases, readings, datasets and mid-semester guest talks.

Incubator as the ecosystem

The CUD Incubator publishes a Problem Bank of challenges solved in the UAE and worldwide. Students pick from it, and Demo Day winners return to it.
