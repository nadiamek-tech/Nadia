Innovation & SDG problem card

Easy version
  1. 1Make: one card.
  2. 2How: one innovation + one real problem.
  3. 3Done when: all four parts are filled.
Draw itSay itWrite it

One innovation you admire (what changed, which of the 4Ps) and one real problem from the SDG Problem Hunt, mapped to the triple bottom line.

  2. Bring one problem from the Problem Hunt.
  3. Map it: people, planet, profit.
