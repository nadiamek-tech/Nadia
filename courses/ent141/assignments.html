Assignments

Four assignments of 25%. Each one follows a block of study and is created in class, so you practise with the instructor present.

Guides

A1 · Week 4 · 25% · Individual · CLO 1, 4

Innovation & SDG problem card

One innovation you admire (what changed, which of the 4Ps) and one real problem from the SDG Problem Hunt, mapped to the triple bottom line.

  1. Pick an innovation: what changed, which of the 4Ps.
  2. Bring one problem from the Problem Hunt.
  3. Map it: people, planet, profit.
  4. Link one SDG.

Tools: Card template (in class) or Canva

Research defense

Interview evidence, Evidence Wall insights and an evidence-based persona, defended in class.

  3. Cluster quotes on the Evidence Wall (Wk 6).
  4. Build and defend the persona (Wk 7–8).

A3 · Week 13 · 25% · Team · CLO 3

Prototype poster + gallery walk

HMW question, idea tournament result and a paper prototype on a poster, shown at the Gallery Walk.
