3.Course Details
3.1Course Description

The course develops an entrepreneurial mindset in first-year students. Students learn to notice real problems on campus, in the UAE and in the CUD Incubator's Problem Bank, and to understand them through observation, guided interviews and the design thinking process. Every session pairs a short concept with studio work in class. Students create four pieces of work, one per learning outcome, ending with a low-fidelity prototype shown at a poster fair. Validation, surveys and pitching are developed in ENT 142.

9.Assessment Methodology

Each assignment follows a block of study and is created in class, so every student practises with the instructor present.

A1 – Innovation card (Individual, Week 4): One UAE product: what changed, which innovation type, what value it creates.

A2 – Problem card (Team, Week 7): The chosen incubator challenge: who is affected, triple bottom line, linked SDG.

A3 – Insight board (Team, Week 11): Empathy map, journey map, HMW question, three ideas and the one chosen.

A4 – Prototype poster + fair (Team, Week 14): Low-fidelity prototype on a poster, shown at the fair, with the teamwork log.
