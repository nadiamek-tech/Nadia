3.Course Details
3.1Course Description

The course builds an entrepreneurial mindset and the skills to discover and design. Students find real problems on campus, in the UAE and through the CUD Incubator's challenge cards, research them through observation and interviews, and build evidence-based personas. They use design thinking, associating, biomimicry and open innovation to generate and select ideas, turn one concept into a paper prototype, and present it at the Innovation Challenge Final with a 90-day roadmap. Each week has a 1-hour lecture and a 2-hour lab. Business models and market analysis follow in ENT 241.

5.Teaching Pedagogy

The course is taught as a weekly 1-hour lecture and a 2-hour lab (1-2-2). In the lecture, the instructor presents one concept from the textbook with real UAE examples and the framework applied step by step. In the lab, students apply it in one hands-on activity (Section 4.1), in teams with rotating roles (leader, timekeeper, recorder, speaker), and keep a teamwork log.

Some lectures or labs may include a short contribution from a practising entrepreneur or a CUD Incubator partner, within the scheduled class time; this does not add contact hours and does not replace the lecture or the lab.

Students are encouraged to take part in hackathons, innovation challenges and competitions at CUD and UAE level.

Inclusive learning: Materials follow Universal Design for Learning. An Easy version of every lecture, lab, worksheet and assignment gives short numbered steps, a sentence starter, read-aloud and one part at a time, and students may show their learning by drawing, speaking or writing. Individual adjustments follow CUD's student-support process.

9.Assessment Methodology

All four assignments follow a block of study and are created in class, so every student practises with the instructor present.

A1 – Innovation & SDG problem card (Individual, Week 4, 25%): One innovation you admire (what changed, which of the 4Ps) and one real problem from the SDG Problem Hunt, mapped to the triple bottom line.

A2 – Research evidence pack (Team, Week 8, 25%): Interview notes, Evidence Wall insights and an evidence-based persona, completed in writing in class. No presentation.

A3 – Prototype poster + gallery walk (Team, Week 13, 25%): HMW question, idea tournament result and a paper prototype on a poster, shown at the Gallery Walk.

A4 – Innovation Challenge Final (Team, Week 16, 25%): Problem → evidence → user → ideas → prototype → feedback → 90-day roadmap, presented with the teamwork log.
