3.Course Details
3.1Course Description

Entrepreneurship 2 is the final course of the CUD entrepreneurship pathway (ENT 141, 142, 241 and 242). Students take the venture planned in ENT 241 to the market. They validate the opportunity and the unique selling proposition, position the venture against competitors, choose a UAE licensing and legal route, plan growth and the team, and audit every business-plan claim against evidence. At a Demo Day marketplace, teams test their offering with real visitors and decide whether to keep, change or pivot. The course ends with an investor panel, a founder journey portfolio across the four ENT courses and a final decision to continue, incubate, pivot or stop, with links to the CUD Incubator. Each week is a 2-hour lab.

5.Teaching Pedagogy

The course is taught as one 2-hour lab each week (0-2-1). Each lab opens with a short concept from the readings and continues with one hands-on challenge (Section 4.1). Teams continue the venture planned in ENT 241 and test it in the real market at the Demo Day (Week 11).

External or CUD Incubator assessors, entrepreneurs and investors may take part in the Demo Day and the investor panel, within the scheduled lab time; this does not add contact hours and does not replace the lab.

Strong ventures are referred to the CUD Incubator, competitions or other university entrepreneurship support after the course.

Inclusive learning: Materials follow Universal Design for Learning: templates, short steps and sentence starters for every in-class challenge, and students may show their learning in writing, speaking or visuals. Individual adjustments follow CUD's student-support process.

9.Assessment Methodology

Four assignments of 25%, one after each block of study. Each one is built in the in-class challenges, so students work on it with the instructor present. Teams of 4–5 keep the same venture across ENT 241 and ENT 242; individual marks come from individual questions and peer evaluation.

A1 – Opportunity validation brief (Team, Week 4, 25%): Why now and what evidence shows a real market; the USP in one sentence, as defended in the USP Battle; and a competitor positioning map that tests the market gap.

A2 – Validation report (Team, Week 8, 25%): Every major claim of the ENT 241 business plan marked Validated / Partially Validated / Assumption, with its evidence; the chosen UAE licensing and legal route; the growth option; and the team plan Now → First Hire → 10 Employees. Up to 3000 words.

A3 – Market evidence & decision (Team, Week 12, 25%): Structured evidence from the Demo Day marketplace (what visitors did and said, objections, reactions to price), the Keep / Change / Pivot / Revalidate decision and the revised plan.

A4 – Investor panel + founder journey portfolio (Team pitch (Week 14), individual portfolio, Week 15, 25%): The venture pitched to an investor or entrepreneur panel, showing what changed because of market evidence, with individual questions; and an individual portfolio of up to 2000 words mapping the journey across ENT 141 → 142 → 241 → 242: GET2 test results, a ranked personal SWOT and a 3–5-year personal development plan, with reflection models and APA references.

Note: A student will lose 10% or more for non-compliance with group efforts (teamwork) and failing to meet tutor deadlines. No late project will be accepted. Exceptions for late evaluation are up to the discretion of the instructor.
