Definition

Minimum viable product

The version of a new product that gives the most validated learning about customers for the least effort.

Ries, The Lean Startup (2011)

Framework

Choose your MVP in five steps

Work from your riskiest assumption, not from the features you like.

  1. Riskiest assumption: what must be true for this to work?
  2. MVP type: video, landing page, concierge or one feature.
  3. Success metric, set before the test.
  4. Test with five real users from the partner.
  5. Decide: persevere, change or stop.
Interpret

Effort vs learning

A cheap MVP that answers the key question beats an expensive one that answers nothing.

Teardown

Your turn: take one apart

In the lab, each team studies an incubator startup's first MVP: what they built, what they left out, what they learned.
