Teardown: ReCup's first MVP

DEMO · fictional incubator partners ReCup solved Bean There Café's cup-waste challenge.

1 · What did they build first?

A QR sticker on 50 reusable cups and a Microsoft Form to log returns. No app.

2 · What did they leave out?

Payments, a mobile app, rewards, other branches.

3 · What did users teach them?

Customers returned cups only when the drop box was by the door. They moved it and returns doubled.

Same course, more ways in

Built on the three UDL principles (CAST, UDL Guidelines 3.0). Adjustments for individual students follow CUD's student-support process.

EngagementWhy learn: real problems, team roles, choice.
RepresentationHow to see it: pictures, simple words, read-aloud.
Action & expressionHow to show it: draw, say or write.

01Same idea, simpler

Every key concept has an Easy-view version: short words and a picture chain.

Standard
MVP: a very early prototype of a product, used to learn from customers.
Easy view
MVP = a small test version. We show it to users and learn.
Small version→Users try it→We learn

02Visual schedule

The session plan as pictures, shown at the start, so nothing is a surprise.

Big tasks are cut into ticks. A visual timer shows time left.

The same learning outcome, three ways to show it. The rubric marks the idea, not the format.
